Identification looks wrong?

Try a sharp, well-lit photo showing distinctive leaves, flowers, stems or growth pattern. Similar species, immature plants, poor focus and partial images can reduce AI accuracy.

Sick-leaf result unclear?

Visible symptoms often have multiple causes. If the plant is valuable, symptoms are severe or the issue may involve regulated pests or pesticide use, consult a qualified horticultural, agricultural or pest-control professional.

Reminder not firing?

Check that notifications are enabled for Plant ID in iOS Settings. System notification settings, Focus modes or device state can affect delivery.

Before acting on treatment advice

AI care guidance is informational. Always check product labels, local rules and plant-specific requirements before applying fertilizer, pesticide, fungicide or other treatment.
