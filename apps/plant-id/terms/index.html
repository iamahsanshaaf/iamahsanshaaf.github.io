Plant identification disclaimer

No guaranteed identification

AI-based plant identification can be wrong, especially where species look similar, the image is incomplete, the plant is immature, lighting is poor or the model lacks sufficient context.

Do not rely on the App alone to identify plants for eating, medicinal use, toxicity determinations, pet safety, livestock safety, legal/regulatory decisions or other high-risk purposes.

Plant-health and disease disclaimer

Plant-health scan results describe possible causes based on visible symptoms and the information available to the App. They are not a guaranteed diagnosis.

Similar symptoms can result from pests, disease, watering issues, nutrient problems, soil conditions, root damage, sunlight stress, temperature, humidity, chemicals or other causes that cannot be confirmed from a photo alone.

For valuable crops, serious infestations, regulated pests, pesticide decisions, invasive species concerns or repeated plant decline, consult a qualified horticultural, botanical, agricultural, nursery, extension-service or pest-control professional as appropriate.
