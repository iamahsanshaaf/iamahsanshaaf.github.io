Product principles

Clear products. Transparent expectations.

We aim to make it easy to understand what each app does, how it is monetized, and where to get help.

01

Useful functionality

Products are designed around focused utility rather than unnecessary complexity.

02

Transparent monetization

Apps may include advertising and optional in-app purchases, as disclosed on their App Store listings.

03

Privacy information

Privacy practices vary by app and are disclosed on each App Store listing and its linked privacy policy.

04

Accessible support

Users can reach the developer directly for product questions, troubleshooting, or feedback.

Transparency

Clear information before you download.

Some apps are free to download and may contain advertising or optional in-app purchases. Exact features, subscription options, privacy practices, and availability can vary by product and region.
