What the App provides

03

How detection works

The App uses the iPhone’s magnetic-field sensors. It does not use the camera to detect metal.

The sensor measures changes in the local magnetic field. A stronger reading or anomaly does not by itself establish the exact object, material, depth, size, location or safety condition causing the reading.

Not every metal produces a strong magnetic response

Results depend heavily on the object’s magnetic properties, distance from the phone, orientation, surrounding electronics, magnets, steel structures, device sensor characteristics and calibration.

04

Calibration and environmental interference

Calibration guidance is intended to improve consistency, not to guarantee accuracy. Nearby speakers, chargers, magnets, electronics, vehicles, structural steel, magnetic accessories, cases and environmental interference can affect readings.

If readings appear unstable, move away from known interference, recalibrate as instructed and compare multiple scans rather than relying on a single value.

05

Metal Scanner, Object Scanner and Electronics Scan

Scan modes organize readings for different casual-use scenarios. Their names describe the user workflow; they do not convert the iPhone into specialized professional detection equipment.

Electronics Scan can indicate changes in magnetic-field readings near electronics or magnetic components, but it cannot diagnose electrical faults, live wiring, electromagnetic safety, device defects or hazardous conditions.

06

Surface Sweep and heatmap-style results

Surface Sweep organizes multiple readings across a user-guided grid. Heatmap-style colors and anomaly markers are visual summaries of recorded magnetic-field readings.

They are not x-ray, radar, ultrasound or photographic images of what exists behind a wall, floor, surface or object. They do not establish exact depth, dimensions or material composition.

07

Peak, anomaly and signal-quality feedback

Peak and anomaly detection highlights relative changes in sensor readings. Signal-quality feedback reflects the App’s interpretation of scan consistency and movement conditions.

These indicators are aids for comparison and exploration only. They are not certified measurements or professional inspection results.
