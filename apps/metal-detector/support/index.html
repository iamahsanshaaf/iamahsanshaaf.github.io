Readings look unstable?

Move away from magnets, speakers, chargers, magnetic phone accessories, electronics and large steel objects. Re-run calibration and compare several readings in a cleaner environment.

Surface Sweep looks inconsistent?

Move the phone smoothly, keep a similar height and orientation, follow the guided grid and avoid changing cases/accessories during the sweep. Recalibrate if the baseline changes significantly.

No signal near an object?

The iPhone magnetometer responds to magnetic-field changes, not every type of metal equally. Object material, distance, size, orientation and environmental interference strongly affect the result.

Subscription or purchase issue?

Premium is billed through Apple. If a purchase is pending, Premium activates only after Apple completes it. Use Restore Purchases for eligible subscriptions and manage cancellation through Apple ID subscription settings.

Need a professional inspection?

Do not rely on this App for construction, electrical, medical, safety or security decisions. Use the appropriate professional inspection equipment and qualified specialist for those scenarios.
