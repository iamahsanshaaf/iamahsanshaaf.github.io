Important sensor disclaimer

Metal Detector measures changes in the iPhone’s magnetic-field sensors. It does not use the camera to detect metal, does not determine exact material composition, and is not a professional construction, electrical, medical, safety or security instrument. Results depend on device sensor quality, object type, distance, calibration, orientation, interference and environment.
