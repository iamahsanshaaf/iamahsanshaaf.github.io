Location not working?

Open iOS Settings and confirm WayMind has location access while using the App. If available, enable Precise Location for more accurate nearby results and route context.

Route or ETA looks wrong?

Refresh the route, confirm your start and destination, check network connectivity and compare against current road conditions. Map and traffic information can change quickly.

Nearby place missing or outdated?

Nearby search depends on map/place data. Business hours, parking, EV charging and other place details may be incomplete or stale. Confirm important details directly with the location.

Reminder not firing?

Check that notifications are enabled for WayMind in iOS Settings. Focus modes, system notification settings and device state can affect delivery.

Subscription or purchase issue?

Premium purchases are billed through Apple. Confirm you are signed into the purchasing Apple ID, use Restore Purchases where available and manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple ID subscription settings.
