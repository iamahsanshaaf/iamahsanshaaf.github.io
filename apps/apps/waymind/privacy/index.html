01

Scope and developer

This Privacy Policy applies to WayMind: Smart GPS & Trips, published by Muhammad Ahsan Shaaf. It explains how information may be processed when you use live location, route planning, nearby places, Discover, saved journeys, saved places, notes, reminders, Ask WayMind, advertising, analytics and optional Premium features.

WayMind is designed around a local-first model: personal trip organization, saved places, recent journeys and related app preferences are intended to remain on your device unless a feature requires network processing or you deliberately export/share information.

02

Location information

WayMind may request access to your device location while you are using the App. Location is used to show your current position, estimate travel times, calculate or preview routes, discover nearby places, support trip planning and improve location-aware features.

Where you request route, map, search or nearby-place functionality, location-related requests may be processed through Apple frameworks and services such as MapKit and Apple Maps. Apple’s handling of those requests is governed by Apple’s applicable privacy terms.

WayMind does not need continuous background location merely to exist as a trip-planning app. Any background or extended location use should only be enabled where the production feature specifically requires it and where iOS permission and App Store disclosures support that use.

03

Saved journeys, places and local storage

WayMind may store journeys, recent routes, saved places, routines, preparation preferences, notes, travel mode, parking context and other planning information locally on your iPhone so the App can restore your personalized travel context.

Deleting saved content inside the App or removing the App may remove locally stored information, subject to your device backup configuration and any explicit export/backup feature you choose to use.

04

Nearby places and place discovery

Nearby-place features may request information about categories such as food, cafés, hotels, fuel, pharmacies, hospitals, ATMs, mechanics, shopping, transit, parking and EV charging.

Search terms, map region, approximate or precise location and selected place details may need to be sent to Apple map/place services to return relevant results.

05

Ask WayMind and planning text

If you use Ask WayMind or another intelligent planning feature, the text you enter must be processed to produce the requested response. Depending on device capability and the production feature configuration, processing may occur locally, through Apple system intelligence services, or through another service provider acting on behalf of the App.

Production consistency

If prompts, notes or planning text are retained on a server, linked to an identifier, reused for model training or processed by a third-party AI provider, the live App Store privacy disclosure and this policy should state that clearly.

06

Advertising and consent

WayMind may display advertising in the free experience using Google AdMob or related Google advertising services. Advertising providers may process device identifiers, ad interaction data, consent signals, approximate technical information and related data as permitted by iOS, your consent choices and applicable law.

Where Apple’s AppTrackingTransparency framework applies, tracking-related processing should follow the authorization choice you make. Where required, a consent-management flow such as Google UMP may be used.

Google privacy information

Google’s privacy information is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

07

Analytics, crash reporting and Remote Config

WayMind may use Firebase services such as Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config to understand app usage, diagnose crashes, evaluate feature reliability, configure non-sensitive app behavior and improve the product.

The exact Firebase modules enabled in production should match both the App Store privacy disclosure and this policy. If a Firebase service is removed or added, the disclosures should be updated accordingly.

08

Subscriptions and StoreKit

WayMind may offer optional auto-renewable subscriptions or other in-app purchases. Purchases are processed through Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your complete payment-card information.

We may receive transaction identifiers, entitlement state, product identifiers, renewal status and related purchase information needed to unlock Premium features, restore purchases, prevent fraud and provide billing support.

09

Notifications, reminders and Live Activities

WayMind may request permission to send journey reminders, preparation alerts, departure notifications or related travel updates. You can allow, deny or later change notification permission in iOS Settings.

If Live Activities or similar system features are available and enabled, the App may display trip-related status on supported system surfaces. These features depend on iOS permissions and device capabilities.

10

Siri, Search and Apple system integrations

If you enable system integrations such as Siri, Search or Apple Intelligence features, iOS may process information according to Apple’s platform rules and privacy practices. WayMind should only expose the minimum app data needed for the enabled integration.

11

Support communications

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, device model, iOS version, app version, screenshots, route/trip context, purchase details and the contents of your support message.

12

Service providers and sharing

WayMind may rely on Apple for maps, routing, search, system intelligence, StoreKit and platform services; Google for advertising and consent; Firebase for analytics, crash reporting or configuration; and other technical infrastructure where required.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law, legal process, security, fraud prevention or to protect legal rights and users.

13

Retention and deletion

Local-first trip data generally remains on your device until you delete it in the App, reset app data or remove the App, subject to device backup behavior.

Third-party service providers may retain information according to their own documented retention practices and the production configuration used by WayMind.

You may request deletion of personal information we control by contacting ahsanshaaf@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, accounting, legal compliance or legitimate dispute resolution.

14

Your choices and rights

  • Location: manage precise/approximate and “While Using” location permission in iOS Settings.
  • Notifications: manage reminder and travel-notification permission in iOS Settings.
  • Tracking: manage tracking authorization in iOS privacy settings where available.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel Premium through Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Saved data: delete journeys, places, history or other local data through the App where controls are provided.
  • Access / deletion requests: contact ahsanshaaf@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live, applicable privacy law may provide additional rights such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability.

15

Children's privacy

WayMind is a general navigation and travel-planning utility and is not designed as a child-directed service. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

16

Security and international processing

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the information processed. No online or location-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for cross-border processing.

17

We may update this policy when WayMind’s location behavior, advertising, Firebase configuration, intelligent features, subscriptions, system integrations or legal requirements change.
