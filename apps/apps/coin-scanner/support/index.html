Scan result looks wrong?

Try clearer lighting, a clean background, sharp focus, and unobstructed photos of both sides. Worn, damaged, reflective, toned, counterfeit, or unusual coins can reduce AI accuracy.

Need an official grade or appraisal?

The App provides collector-oriented estimates only. For official grading, authentication, attribution, or appraisal, use a qualified numismatic professional or recognized grading service.
